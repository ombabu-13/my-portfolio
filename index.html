About

Not finished. Actively under construction.

I'm early in this. No decade of experience, no long résumé of companies — just a computer science student from Nepal, now studying at SIT Pune, trying to connect a fairly unusual set of experiences into one direction.

Before code, I was already doing detail-heavy work: organizing invoices and packing lists for my family's business, helping process vehicle transit paperwork at the Nepal border, running a stall at a local carnival. None of it was software. All of it taught me that accuracy and follow-through matter before you ever touch a keyboard.

Computer science is where I'm now pointing that instinct — one course, one project, one broken build at a time.

Journey

How I got to a computer science degree by way of customs paperwork

Roughly chronological. Told straight, no embellishment.

01
Origin

Nepal

Grew up in Nepal — through 10th grade at Everest Secondary School, then +2 Science with Computer Science at Shikshadeep Higher Secondary School, before moving to India for my undergraduate degree.

02
Real world, early

Family business & the border

Worked as a documentation assistant at Krishna Enterprises — commercial invoices, packing lists, proforma invoices — and assisted multiple times with Vehicle Customs Transit System processes for trucks crossing into Nepal. Unusual exposure for a CS student's backstory, but it's where I first learned that a misplaced number in a document is a real problem, not a typo.

03
Side venture

The carnival stall

Designed and ran a game-based stall at a local carnival — pricing it, managing cash flow, pulling in customers, and actually turning a profit. Small-scale, but it was my first real lesson in how a system (any system) has to hold up under real people and real money.
